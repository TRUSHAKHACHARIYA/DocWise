Section 12 — Term and Termination
12.1 Initial Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for twelve (12) months unless terminated earlier under this Section 12.
12.2 Renewal. The Agreement renews automatically for successive one (1) year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.
12.3 Termination for Convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) days prior written notice to the other party.
12.4 Early Termination Fee. If Customer terminates for convenience before the end of an Initial Term or Renewal Term, Customer shall pay a pro-rated early termination fee equal to fifty percent (50%) of the remaining fees for that term, as detailed in Exhibit B.
12.5 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within thirty (30) days of notice.

Section 14 — Limitation of Liability
14.1 Except for breaches of confidentiality, indemnification obligations, or gross negligence, each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
14.2 Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

Exhibit B — Fee Schedule
B.1 Annual platform fee: $24,000 payable quarterly in advance.
B.2 Early termination fee calculation: 50% of unpaid fees for the remaining contract period.

Section 8 — Data Protection
8.1 Vendor shall maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards consistent with SOC 2 Type II standards.
8.2 Customer data shall be retained for seven (7) years after contract termination unless law requires longer retention.
