INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY (SAMPLE)
DocWise Demo Document — Internal policy excerpt for onboarding.

1. Purpose
This policy defines minimum security controls for employees, contractors, and vendors handling company or customer data.

2. Access Control
2.1 All users must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) for production systems.
2.2 Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed quarterly.
2.3 Shared credentials are prohibited.

3. Vendor Management
3.1 Third-party vendors with access to confidential data must sign a Data Processing Addendum (DPA).
3.2 Vendors must provide evidence of annual penetration testing and vulnerability remediation.
3.3 Critical vendors are subject to annual security questionnaire review.

4. Data Retention and Disposal
4.1 Customer contracts and audit logs are retained for seven (7) years.
4.2 Employees must securely delete local copies of confidential documents within thirty (30) days of project closure.
4.3 Hard drives must be wiped using NIST 800-88 standards before disposal.

5. Incident Response
5.1 Security incidents must be reported to the Security team within one (1) hour of discovery.
5.2 Material breaches affecting customer data require customer notification within seventy-two (72) hours where required by law.

6. Acceptable Use
6.1 Uploading customer data to unapproved AI tools is prohibited without Legal and Security approval.
6.2 Employees must verify AI-generated answers against source documents before use in compliance workflows.
